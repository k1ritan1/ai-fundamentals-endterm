SLIDE 03 // MODULE 1: SEARCH & HEURISTICS

Agent Architectures & Admissible Pathfinding

Theoretical Foundations (Weeks 1-2)
  • PEAS Framework: Performance Measure, Environment, Actuators, Sensors applied to exterior crawler Alpha-1.
  • Uninformed Baselines: BFS (FIFO, complete, optimal for unit costs), DFS (LIFO, linear space), UCS (priority queue by path cost g(n)).
  • Informed A* Search: Evaluation function f(n) = g(n) + h(n). Combines backward path cost with forward heuristic estimate.
Optimality Proofs & Dominance
  • Admissibility: h(n) ≤ h*(n). Guarantees tree search optimality by ensuring suboptimal goals cannot be expanded prematurely.
  • Consistency (Monotonicity): h(n) ≤ c(n, a, n') + h(n'). Satisfies the triangle inequality, guaranteeing graph search optimality without reopening closed nodes.
  • Physical AI Constraint: Crawler operates under 100 ms cycle deadline and 512 KB RAM; memory-bounded search prevents heap overflows.
In Chapter 1, we highlight the formal proof of A* optimality. Many students memorize that heuristics must not overestimate, but in graph search with a closed set, admissibility alone is insufficient: consistency is mandatory to prevent reopening nodes. We demonstrate this on the exterior hull grid where track slip and battery draw dictate path choices.
SLIDE 04 // MODULE 1: LOCAL SEARCH & CSPS

Power Grid CSP & Combinatorial Optimization

Local Optimization (Week 3)
  • Hill Climbing Failures: Local maxima, flat plateaus, and knife-edge ridges stall greedy gradient ascent.
  • Simulated Annealing: Escapes local traps using Boltzmann acceptance probability P(accept) = exp(-ΔE / T). As temperature T decays, convergence to global optima is guaranteed asymptotically.
Constraint Satisfaction Problems (CSPs)
  • Formal Tuple: Variables X (subsystem buses), Domains D (voltage levels), Constraints C (bus capacity ≤ 8 kW, life support ≥ 2 kW).
  • Arc Consistency (AC-3): Prunes invalid domain values in O(cd^3) time prior to and during search.
  • Ordering Heuristics: MRV ("fail-first") selects the most constrained variable; LCV ("fail-last") orders values to maximize flexibility.
In Chapter 2, we contrast local optimization with systematic constraint programming. Hill climbing is fast but brittle under bus-bar overload. CSPs with AC-3 arc consistency provide formal guarantees that life-support power floors are never violated, protecting sleeping crew members from transformer brownouts.
SLIDE 05 // MODULE 2: ADVERSARIAL DEFENSE

Game Trees & Alpha-Beta Pruning Efficiency

Adversarial Formulation (Week 4)
  • Zero-Sum Assumption: Utility(MAX) + Utility(MIN) = 0. Defense sentry maximizes survival; intruder scavenger minimizes vessel integrity.
  • Minimax Recursion: Explores state space in O(b^m) time. Requires depth cutoffs and linear weighted evaluation functions V(s) = f(w) in real-time combat.
  • Expectimax Extension: Handles stochastic environmental events (venting steam, weapon jam) through expected value over chance nodes.
Alpha-Beta Pruning Mathematical Proof
  • Alpha (α): Highest-value choice found so far along path for MAX (initialized to -∞).
  • Beta (β): Lowest-value choice found so far along path for MIN (initialized to +∞).
  • Pruning Condition: When α ≥ β, branch is cut immediately.
  • Complexity Reduction: Under optimal move ordering, complexity drops from O(b^m) to O(b^{m/2}), doubling effective lookahead depth.
Chapter 3 covers adversarial games. We provide the complete mathematical proof showing why alpha-beta pruning is strictly sound: once a MIN node drops below alpha, the parent MAX ancestor already has a superior option, rendering further exploration mathematically irrelevant. In the simulation, you can see 58% to 91% of tree nodes pruned in real time.
SLIDE 06 // MODULE 3: MDPS & REINFORCEMENT LEARNING

Tokamak Coolant MDPs & Model-Free Q-Learning

Markov Decision Processes (Week 5)
  • Markov Property: Future states depend solely on current state and action, independent of history.
  • Bellman Optimality Equations: Decomposes optimal value V*(s) and Q*(s, a) into immediate reward plus discounted maximum future return.
  • Value Iteration: Proven convergence via Banach fixed-point theorem; the Bellman operator is a γ-contraction mapping in L_∞ norm.
Temporal Difference Q-Learning
  • Model-Free Control: Learns directly from sampled transitions (s, a, r, s') without knowing transition probabilities T(s, a, s').
  • Update Rule: Q(s, a) ← Q(s, a) + α [r + γ max Q(s', a') - Q(s, a)].
  • Physical AI Challenge: Pure ε-greedy exploration risks reactor meltdown; safe reinforcement learning mandates control barrier safety envelopes.
Chapter 4 addresses sequential decision-making under delayed rewards. We derive the Bellman optimality equations and demonstrate both model-based Value Iteration and model-free Q-learning. We specifically emphasize the Physical AI danger of unconstrained exploration: an exploratory action in a software simulation costs nothing, but in a fusion tokamak, it triggers physical magnetic quench.
SLIDE 07 // MODULE 3: PROBABILISTIC TRACKING

Bayesian Networks & Sequential HMM Filtering

Probabilistic Foundations (Weeks 6-7)
  • Axioms of Probability: Kolmogorov axioms, joint distributions, marginalization, and Bayes' rule P(Cause|Effect) = α P(Effect|Cause) P(Cause).
  • Bayesian Networks: Factoring joint distributions using DAG topology and conditional independence: P(X_1...X_n) = ∏ P(X_i | Parents(X_i)).
  • d-separation: Blocking rules across linear chains, common causes, and v-structures / colliders ("explaining away").
Hidden Markov Model Forward Filtering
  • Hidden State X_t & Observation E_t: State transition model P(X_t | X_{t-1}) and sensor emission model P(E_t | X_t).
  • Recursive Forward Equation: P(X_t | e_{1:t}) = α P(e_t | X_t) ∑ P(X_t | x_{t-1}) P(x_{t-1} | e_{1:t-1}).
  • Matrix Formulation: f_{1:t} = α O_t T^T f_{1:t-1}. Runs in O(S^2) time, enabling real-time acoustic leak tracking despite 25% sensor noise.
Chapter 5 establishes that classical logic fails in noisy physical environments. We derive Bayes' rule and step through the recursive forward filtering equation for Hidden Markov Models. In the simulation, students can inject noisy acoustic pings and watch the probability distribution collapse onto the true physical fracture location.
SLIDE 08 // MODULE 4: ML & UTILITY

Atmospheric Diagnostics & Cost-Sensitive Utility

Utility Theory & Classifiers (Week 8)
  • Von Neumann-Morgenstern Utility: Axiomatic rationality under risk; Maximum Expected Utility (MEU) principle. Concave utility reflects mandatory life-support risk aversion.
  • Naive Bayes: Conditional independence assumption P(X|Y) = ∏ P(X_i|Y). Laplace smoothing (+1 pseudocounts) prevents zero-frequency collapse.
  • Linear Classifiers: Sigmoid activation σ(z) = 1 / (1 + e^{-z}) mapping linear scores to posterior breach probabilities.
Cost-Sensitive Confusion Matrix Optimization
  • Diagnostic Metrics: Precision = TP/(TP+FP), Recall = TP/(TP+FN), F1-Score = 2PR/(P+R).
  • Life-Critical Asymmetry: False Negative (Cost_FN = 100) is far worse than False Positive (Cost_FP = 1). Net value V(Predict 1).
  • Optimal Decision Threshold: τ* = Cost_FP / [Cost_FP + Cost_FN] ≈ 0.01. Tuning the operational threshold eliminates fatalities while managing false alarms.
Chapter 6 bridges statistical machine learning and decision theory. In physical life support, accuracy is a dangerously misleading metric. By formalizing a cost matrix where a False Negative carries 100 times the penalty of a False Positive, we prove why the optimal classification threshold must be shifted from 0.5 down to 0.1, guaranteeing zero crew asphyxiations.
SLIDE 09 // MODULE 4: NEURAL NETS & SAFETY

Docking Guidance Networks, Attention, & Alignment

Neural Foundations (Weeks 9-10)
  • Perceptrons & XOR Barrier: McCulloch-Pitts logic, Rosenblatt learning rule, and Minsky-Papert proof of linear separability limits.
  • Multi-Layer Perceptrons (MLPs): Non-linear activations (ReLU, Sigmoid, Tanh), Universal Approximation Theorem, and Backpropagation calculus via multivariable chain rule.
  • Transformer Self-Attention: Attention(Q, K, V) = softmax(QK^T / √d_k) V. Scaled dot-product mechanism eliminates recurrent bottlenecks.
AI Safety & Constitutional Alignment
  • Specification Gaming / Reward Hacking: AI exploits reward definitions through destructive shortcuts (e.g. venting atmosphere to halt thermal rise).
  • Goodhart's Law: When a proxy measure becomes a target, it ceases to be a reliable measure.
  • Hardware Interlocks: Constitutional AI filters coupled with physical relay disconnects that physically sever power to unsafe thrusters regardless of model output.
In Chapter 7, we progress from McCulloch-Pitts neurons and the XOR barrier to modern Multi-Layer Perceptrons and Transformer self-attention.
